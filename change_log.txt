Proposed changes:

   - app_version

   - Remove the default value "http://127.0.0.1:8010".
   - Keep knowledge_base_url as a required runtime setting.
   - Keep main.py as the normal source of KNOWLEDGE_BASE_URL injection.

   - Delete RetrievalPipeline.retrieve_domain.
   - Delete EvidenceAdapter.from_domain_chunk.
   - Remove DataDomainWorkflow local Knowledge Base scheduling and execution.
   - Remove DomainProfile.knowledge_index and its None values in domain profiles.

   - Replace index_aliases = {"policy": "tender_qa_policy"} with policy_collection_alias = "tender_qa_policy".
   - Use policy_collection_alias in KnowledgeBaseService.search and rebuild.py.

   - Delete request.index == "policy" checks.
